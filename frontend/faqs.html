Your Questions, Answered

Here are frequently asked questions about the COVID-19 pandemic across the country and provinces.

National Provinces

What are the current national COVID-19 safety guidelines?

Guidelines include wearing masks indoors, maintaining social distancing, and regular hand hygiene.

Are vaccines still effective against new COVID-19 variants?

Yes, updated vaccines are effective at reducing severe illness and hospitalizations from new variants.

How can I get tested for COVID-19?

You can visit local health clinics, pharmacies, or mobile testing centers. Check your province's health website for locations.

Is travel allowed between provinces?

Interprovincial travel is allowed, but restrictions may apply during outbreak surges in specific areas.

What should I do if I test positive?

Isolate at home for at least 5 days, inform close contacts, and monitor symptoms. Seek medical help if symptoms worsen.

Which provinces have the highest case numbers currently?

Case numbers vary; refer to the latest update on the COVID-19 dashboard for accurate province-level data.

Are masks mandatory in schools?

Mask mandates depend on the province. Some regions have optional policies, others require masks indoors.

How can I track COVID-19 trends in my province?

Use official government COVID-19 trackers or our interactive dashboard with map and graph views.

What is the status of the vaccination drive?

Vaccination drives are ongoing nationwide, including booster campaigns targeting at-risk populations.

Are there any restrictions for public gatherings?

Restrictions vary by region and case severity. Always check local health advisories before planning events.

Is COVID-19 testing still free?

Yes, in most provinces testing remains free at public facilities. Private centers may charge a fee.
